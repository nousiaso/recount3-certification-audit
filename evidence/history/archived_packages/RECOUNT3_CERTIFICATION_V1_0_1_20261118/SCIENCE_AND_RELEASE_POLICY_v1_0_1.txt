RECOUNT3 SCIENTIFIC RELEASE POLICY v1.0.1

Evidence lock (v1.0.0): retained, unmodified, SHA256 verified.
Separate cohort v1.0.1: only primary-tumour specimen for the primary cohort,
  with exact accession evidence, study reference, reviewer, positive patient
  mapping, resolved within-patient repeated-sampling status, retained run(s),
  and no discordant retained technical-run pair.
Technical runs: no inclusion merely by 0.9 correlation if the run has other
  unexplained QC warnings; human adjudication remains required.
Exclusions: 37 known DGE experiment libraries are immutable exclusions.
Biological analyses: use patient-clustered methods for multiregion/longitudinal
  repeated samples. Cross-study sample counts are not automatically patient counts.
Cross-atlas: absence of a known match from partial 740/9465 linkage is not
  sufficient proof of independence. Same-source 143 lung specimens are
  calibration-only, never independent external validation.
Model: the original 499 W features and three branches must be preserved;
  465/499 identity agreement can support descriptive assay diagnostics only.
Cross-platform transfer: high expression correlation is not programme/ecotype
  score equivalence. Actual frozen-code scores, source processing, and independent
  study evidence are needed before any pooled or comparative model release.
Limitations: source study records and reviewer attestations are not independently
  fetched by v1.0.1; they must be supplied and audited. HOLD is correct otherwise.
